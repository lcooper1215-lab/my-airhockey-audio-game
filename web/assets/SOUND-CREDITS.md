# Sound credits

Isolated, filtered and faded table impacts are derived from the publicly available high-quality MP3 preview of **air hockey smack.WAV**, by **krb21**.

Source: https://freesound.org/people/krb21/sounds/118604/
Preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/118/118604_1269383-hq.mp3
License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Retrieved 2026-10-16. Source recording describes paddle and wall impacts during a real air hockey game. Short edited impacts are used for table-hit textures; the rail preset layers the bright impact with synthesized inharmonic metallic ringing.

Airflow, fan motor, positional puck clicks, and the other selectable sound textures are synthesized locally. No background music or external streaming is used.
